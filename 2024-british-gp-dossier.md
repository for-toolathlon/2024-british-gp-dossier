## 1. Official Storyline (from race records)

- **Round:** 11th round of the 2024 season. Date: 2024-07-07. Race details: Hamilton won his first Silverstone win since 2021 Saudi Arabian GP — his 104th career victory — and his record ninth British GP win.
- **Top three:** 1. Lewis Hamilton (Mercedes) ; 2. Max Verstappen (Red Bull); 3. Lando Norris (McLaren).
- **Pole:** George Russell (Mercedes) — 1:25.819 (grid 1). At the flag, Russell retired mid-race on lap 34 from a water leak, so Hamilton won behind him.
- **Hamilton's quickest lap:** 1:29.438 (rank 5) on lap 45 of 52 laps.

**Championship context:** Verstappen led the drivers' championship with 237 points entering the race; Mercedes sat 4th in the constructors' championship with 468 of 666.
